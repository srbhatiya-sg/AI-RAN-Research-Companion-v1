# Sample Questions

The following questions can be used as starting points when exploring a research paper with the AI-RAN Research Companion.

They are examples rather than fixed prompts. Adapt them to the document and research objective.

## Understand the Paper

```text
What problem is this paper trying to solve?

Why is this problem important?

What is the main contribution of the paper?

What are the key conclusions?

Explain the paper in a concise brief for a telecom professional who has not read it.

```
## Explore the Technical Approach

```text
What are the key technical concepts introduced in the paper?

Explain the proposed approach step by step.

What technologies, mechanisms, or components are involved?

What assumptions does the proposed approach make?

What technical challenges is the approach trying to address?
```

## Analyze the Architecture

```text
What are the major components of the proposed architecture?

What is the role of each component?

How do the components interact?

What are the key data and control flows?

Which parts of the architecture are existing technologies and which are proposed or emerging?

What would need to change in an existing telecom network to implement this approach?

```

## Examine the Evidence

```text
What evidence does the paper provide to support its key claims?

Which results are based on experiments, simulations, measurements, or analysis?

What are the most important quantitative results?

What limitations does the evaluation have?

Which conclusions are strongly supported by the evidence, and which require further validation?
```

## Critical Review

```text
What are the strongest technical contributions of this paper?

What assumptions could limit the proposed approach?

What are the main technical or deployment challenges?

What limitations are acknowledged by the authors?

What important questions remain unanswered?

What would you challenge or question if you were reviewing this paper?
```
## Compare Multiple Papers
When multiple papers are available in the workspace:
```text
Compare the approaches described in these papers.

What problems are common across the papers?

Where do the technical approaches differ?

What assumptions are different?

Which ideas are complementary?

What common research gaps emerge from the comparison?
```

## Create Research Notes
``` text
Create a concise research brief from this paper.

List the five most important technical insights.

List the key open research questions.

Identify concepts that require further investigation.

Create discussion questions for a technical discussion.

Create a structured set of notes for future research.
```
