# Study Modes

The AI-RAN Research Companion can be used to explore a research paper from different perspectives.

Each study mode applies a different lens to the same research material.

## 1. Executive Mode

### Purpose
Understand the paper quickly from a strategic and high-level perspective.

### Focus
- Problem being addressed
- Why it matters
- Main contribution
- Key findings
- Potential industry impact

### Typical output
A concise executive brief that explains the paper without requiring the reader to go through all of its technical detail.

---

## 2. Researcher Mode

### Purpose
Explore the technical depth and research contribution of the paper.

### Focus
- Research problem
- Technical approach
- Key concepts and mechanisms
- Assumptions
- Evaluation methodology
- Results
- Limitations
- Open research questions

### Typical output
A structured technical analysis that helps identify what the paper contributes and where further research may be needed.

---

## 3. Telecom Architect Mode

### Purpose
Examine the paper from a network architecture and deployment perspective.

### Focus
- Architecture and components
- Interfaces and interactions
- Data and control flows
- Integration with existing networks
- Dependencies
- Deployment considerations
- Practical challenges

### Typical output
An architecture-oriented view of how the proposed ideas could fit into a real telecom environment.

---

## 4. Critical Review Mode

### Purpose
Challenge the ideas presented in the paper rather than simply summarizing them.

### Focus
- Key assumptions
- Strengths and weaknesses
- Evidence supporting the claims
- Technical limitations
- Practical constraints
- Unanswered questions
- Areas requiring further validation

### Typical output
A balanced assessment that separates what the paper demonstrates from what remains an assumption, proposal, or open question.

---

## Using Multiple Modes

The same paper can be explored using multiple modes.

A useful sequence is:

Executive Mode
      ↓
